Shreya Fadia is a writer and editor who lives in the mountains of North Carolina. She holds a JD from Columbia Law School and an MFA from Indiana University, where she served as editor in chief of the *Indiana Review*. She is a 2025 Periplus fellow, and her fiction has appeared in *Black Warrior Review*, *Booth*, *Fourteen Hills*, *The Margins*, *Phoebe*, and elsewhere. She is currently at work on a novel.
